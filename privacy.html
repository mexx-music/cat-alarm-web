Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Cat Alarm does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No user accounts are created or required.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No usage analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Local Storage

The app may store preferences locally on your device (such as selected alarm sound or settings). This data never leaves your device.

Permissions

Cat Alarm requests only the permissions required for core functionality (e.g. audio playback, preventing the screen from sleeping during an active alarm). No sensitive permissions are requested.

Third-Party Services

Cat Alarm does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
